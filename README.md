PRAVAAH — AI-Powered Flood Risk & Emergency Response Platform
PRAVAAH is an AI-driven disaster management platform designed to help authorities **predict flood risks, identify vulnerable areas, prioritize emergency response, and plan resource allocation** before a disaster escalates.
Instead of responding only after people report flooding, PRAVAAH aims to provide **early, data-driven insights** that can support faster and more effective disaster response.

**Problem Statement**

Floods can cause significant damage to lives, infrastructure, and essential services. Traditional disaster response often depends on reports and manual assessment, which can delay decision-making.

PRAVAAH addresses this challenge by combining **flood prediction, risk assessment, vulnerability analysis, and emergency planning** into a unified system.

##  Our Solution

PRAVAAH follows a data-driven pipeline:

**Flood Prediction → Risk Assessment → Vulnerability Analysis → Priority Identification → Emergency Planning**

The platform can help identify:

*  Areas at higher risk of flooding
*  Vulnerable zones requiring greater attention
*  Areas that should receive emergency resources first
*  Suitable emergency response plans
*  Data-driven insights for disaster management authorities

---

##  Key Features

### 1. Flood Prediction

Uses historical and environmental data to predict potential flood risk.

### 2. Risk Assessment

Analyzes predicted flood conditions to generate risk levels for different zones.

### 3. Vulnerability Analysis

Considers zone-level vulnerability to identify areas that may require additional assistance.

### 4. Priority Prediction

Ranks affected or vulnerable areas according to the urgency of intervention.

### 5. Emergency Planning

Generates structured emergency response plans based on predicted risks and priorities.

### 6. Data-Driven Decision Support

Provides actionable information that can assist authorities in planning resources and emergency responses.

##  Workflow

1. Collect relevant flood and zone-level data.
2. Predict potential flood risk using the ML model.
3. Evaluate the risk associated with different zones.
4. Analyze vulnerability of affected areas.
5. Prioritize areas requiring immediate intervention.
6. Generate emergency response and resource allocation plans.
7. Provide actionable outputs for disaster management.
